MARGIN 1.0 - LAUNCH CHECKLIST
A music store simulation. Sign-in, auto-save, help emails and the teacher view work
the same way as Compound, in the same SCHOOL Supabase project.

1. SUPABASE (your SCHOOL project)
   a. pg_net should already be on from Compound (Database > Extensions). If not, turn it on.
   b. SQL Editor > New query: paste ALL of setup.sql and click Run. It should say Success.
      Safe to run again any time. If the paste gets cut off, run it in parts at the
      "END OF PART" lines, in order.

2. PUT THE FILES ONLINE (same place as your other programs)
   GitHub Pages: open the YDTSCHOOL / PORTAL repository, Add file > Upload files, and drag in
   the whole "margin" folder. Commit. After a minute or two the address will be
   https://ydtschool.github.io/PORTAL/margin/
   (Or, once Netlify has credits again: drag the "margin" folder onto Netlify.)

3. SCHOOL PORTAL
   a. Make sure Shlomo is in the portal with a first name and PIN (he already is).
   b. Add a program called Margin with the address from step 2, and tick it for him.

4. TEACHER VIEW (on the Margin sign-in screen, click Teacher, use your portal password)
   a. Help emails: Margin uses Compound's email settings automatically, so they should
      already say "Emails are on". To send Margin's emails to a different address, type it
      there with the Resend key and press Save.
   b. Click Send test email. Check your inbox and spam folder.
   c. Daily lock: OFF to start. Turn it on and set the times if you want Margin to close
      at certain hours, the same as Compound.

5. TEST BEFORE HE STARTS
   a. Sign in as a test student (or as him), name a store, order stock, run a few weeks.
   b. Sign out and sign back in: the store should be exactly where you left it.
   c. Press "Ask my teacher", send a question. You get an email with his store's numbers
      and a REPLY TO HIM HERE link. Reply; he sees it the next time he signs in.
   d. In the Teacher view press Reset on whoever you tested with, so he starts fresh.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THE TEACHER VIEW
  For each student: game week, net worth (and his best ever), goals reached, total profit,
  last week's profit, loan, reputation, time today and this week, and help requests.
  Activity log: every order, offer he took or turned down, bank move, upgrade, and each
  week's result, so you can see his decisions, not just his score.
  Help requests tab: his questions and your replies, with read receipts.

REPLYING TO HIM
  Every help email has a "REPLY TO HIM HERE" link. Click it, enter your teacher password,
  type your reply and press Send reply. (Pressing Reply in your email app does NOT reach him.)

GOOD TO KNOW
  Saves automatically after every action. Signs out after 20 minutes without activity.
  "New store" lets him start over; you'll see it in the log and in the teacher table.
  Try it without signing in: add ?demo to the end of the address (nothing is saved).

FILES
