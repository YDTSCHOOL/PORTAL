COMPOUND 3.4 - LAUNCH CHECKLIST

WHAT CHANGED IN 3.4 (no typed math)
  - No calculation problems anywhere. The math is shown, not asked: in the lesson's worked
    examples, in the walkthroughs, in the Case File stories, and in the worked solution that
    appears after each decision question.
  - Each lesson day has up to 2 quick decision questions (pick an answer, e.g. "Which deal earns
    more?"), then the Case File. Days with no decision questions go straight to the Case File.
  - Weekly quiz and boss rounds use reading and decision questions only.
  - To bring calculations back later, raise PRACTICE_CALC and QUIZ_CALC in core.js.


WHAT CHANGED IN 3.3 (less math, more reading)
  - Practice is now 3 problems a day instead of 10: up to 2 calculations and 1 concept question.
  - New: a Case File every lesson day. A short real-life money story with 5 questions about
    decisions and mistakes, no calculator needed. It counts toward the day's mastery score.
  - Weekly quiz: 4 problems + 6 reading questions from that week's Case Files and Deep Dives.
  - Videos, lessons and Deep Dives are unchanged. No database changes: just upload the files.
  - If he was in the middle of a day's practice when you update, that day's practice restarts.
  - To adjust the mix, change PRACTICE_CALC, PRACTICE_CONCEPT or QUIZ_CALC in core.js.

1. SUPABASE (your SCHOOL project)
   a. Database > Extensions: search "pg_net" and turn it on. (Needed for help emails.)
   b. SQL Editor > New query: paste ALL of setup.sql and click Run. It should say Success.
      Safe to run again any time. If the paste gets cut off, run it in parts at the
      "END OF PART" lines, in order.

2. RESEND (for help emails)
   a. Sign in at resend.com (you already have an account).
   b. API Keys > Create API Key. Copy the key (starts with re_). You only see it once.
   c. Emails can only go to the email address you signed up to Resend with,
      unless you verify your own domain in Resend.

3. NETLIFY
   a. Unzip, then drag the "compound" folder onto Netlify (Add new site > Deploy manually).
   b. Copy the site address (ends in .netlify.app).

4. SCHOOL PORTAL
   a. Make sure the student is in the portal with a first name and PIN.
   b. Add a program called Compound with the Netlify address, and tick it for him.

5. TEACHER VIEW (on the Compound sign-in screen, click Teacher, use your portal password)
   a. Daily lock: should show On, closes 3:10 PM, reopens 4:15 PM. Click Save once.
   b. Help emails: type your Resend sign-up email and paste the re_ key. Save.
   c. Click Send test email. Check your inbox and spam folder.

6. TEST ON HIS CHROMEBOOK (before his first class)
   a. Easiest: add a test student in the portal and sign in as that student.
   b. Go through the orientation, start week 1 day 1, check the video plays.
   c. On a practice question: press Hint, then Set it up, then the gold
      "Still stuck? Email my teacher" button. You should get the email.
   d. Open the email, click the REPLY TO HIM HERE link, enter your teacher password,
      type a reply and press Send reply. Sign in as the student again: the reply
      shows up first, before the lesson.
   e. In the Teacher view, press Reset on the test student when you're done.
   f. If you tested with HIS account instead, press Reset on him so he starts fresh.

7. FIRST DAY
   He signs in, the orientation opens by itself, and week 1 starts when he finishes it.

REPLYING TO HIM
  Every help email has a "REPLY TO HIM HERE" link. Click it, enter your teacher password,
  type your reply and press Send reply. (Pressing Reply in your email app does NOT reach him.)
  You can also reply from the Teacher view: the right side now has a "Help requests" tab.
  He sees your reply the next time he signs in, before his lesson starts, and you can see
  when he has read it.
