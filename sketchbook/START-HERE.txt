SKETCHBOOK: 20 UNITS, 100 ASSIGNMENTS
SETUP IN 4 STEPS  (about 10 minutes, one time only)

IMPORTANT: Steps 1 and 2 connect the progress database. Until you do them, the sign-in
screen says "saving on this computer only" and her work will NOT follow her to another
computer. After them it says "Progress database connected" (green dot).

STEP 1. Add the table to Supabase
  - Go to supabase.com and open your SCHOOL project.
  - Click "SQL Editor" on the left, then "New query".
  - Open the file setup.sql (in this folder) with Notepad, copy everything, paste it in, and press "Run".
  - You should see "Success. No rows returned."

STEP 2. Fill in config.js
  - Open config.js with Notepad.
  - Paste the same Supabase URL and anon key you used in the portal's config.js
    between the quote marks on the SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY lines.
  - Change ADMIN_PASSWORD from teacher to a password you choose.
  - Save the file.

STEP 3. Put the folder on GitHub (same place as the portal)
  - Go to github.com, sign in as YDTSCHOOL and open the PORTAL repository.
  - Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
  - Drag the whole "sketchbook" folder into the box, then press "Commit changes".
  - Wait about 2 minutes. The program is now at:
        https://ydtschool.github.io/PORTAL/sketchbook/
    (If your portal's address looks different, use that address and add /sketchbook/ at the end.)
  - Add that address to the portal as a new program button, or bookmark it on her Chromebook.

STEP 4. Turn on the email reports (once)
  - Open the program, click "Teacher page" at the bottom, type your password.
  - Have the student sign in and answer a few questions, then sign out (or use the
    "Email today's report" button on her page).
  - The first email goes to sfsendlerydt@gmail.com from FormSubmit with an "Activate" button.
    Click it (check Spam). After that, a report arrives every time she finishes.

HOW CHAVI SIGNS IN AND OUT
  She types her first and last name and makes up a 4-digit code the first time.
  Signing in loads her saved progress from the database. Her work also saves after every
  question. When she presses "Sign out" the program saves everything and shows "Saved!".
  The same name and code work on any computer. If she forgets her code,
  open the Teacher page, click her name, and press "Set a new code".

TO PLACE HER AHEAD
  Teacher page > her name > "Open the next unit". Each press opens one more unit.

TO UPDATE LATER
  Upload the new files into the same sketchbook folder on GitHub. Her progress is kept.
